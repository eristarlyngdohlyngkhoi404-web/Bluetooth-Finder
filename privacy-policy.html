Privacy Policy for BT//TRACKER

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Developer: CreepstarKing

Contact: creepstarking@gmail.com

BT//TRACKER ("the app") helps you find your own Bluetooth devices (earbuds, speakers, bands, phones) by using Bluetooth signal strength and your phone's compass. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Short version

  • The app has no account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, receive or store your personal information.
  • Bluetooth scanning, the compass and the voice announcements all work on your phone only.
  • We use ads to keep the app free and our server alive. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect some data for ads, as described below.

What the app can and cannot find

The app can only find devices that are switched on and have Bluetooth enabled. A device that is off, or has its Bluetooth turned off, cannot be found. Distances are estimates based on signal strength and may be several metres off.

Permissions and why the app needs them

  • Bluetooth (scan and connect): to find nearby Bluetooth devices, read their signal strength, and list your paired devices.
  • Location: Android requires the Location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read, use, store or send your location.
  • Motion and compass sensors: to show which way your phone points. This data is used on the phone only.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep searching and speaking range updates while the app is in the background. A notification shows while the app is searching.
  • Internet and network state: to download online themes and to load ads.
  • Text-to-speech: the app uses your phone's built-in voice to say the distance. It does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Information the app uses on your phone

  • Names, addresses and signal strength of nearby Bluetooth devices are used on your phone to build the list and radar. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and are not saved after you close the app, except as below.
  • The app saves your settings (theme, text size, voice and unit options), the themes you unlocked, and the time of the last ad, on your phone only. We cannot see them. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

Online themes

When you open the app, it downloads a small theme list (a text file) from a public GitHub page. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address and basic request details when your phone makes this request. No personal information is sent by the app. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to it.

Advertising

We use ads to keep the app free and to help keep our server alive. The app shows ads using Google AdMob. This includes full-screen ads shown when you open the app and about every 15 minutes while you use it, and optional ads you choose to watch to unlock a theme.

Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The data may be used for personalised ads depending on your settings. See Google's information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (ads) and GitHub (hosting the theme list), as described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Because we do not collect your personal information, we store none of it on our servers. Data the app keeps stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

Responsible use

Use the app only to find your own devices, or devices whose owner agrees. Do not use it to follow or track people.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
